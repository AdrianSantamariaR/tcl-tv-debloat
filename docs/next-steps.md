# Product roadmap

The app previews, applies and restores supported TCL reporting settings and eligible optional packages. Changes require matching firmware and application versions, a fresh scan and a saved recovery journal.

Planned improvements include additional supported profiles and clearer guidance for retaining TV functions. New controls require verified behavior and exact restoration before becoming selectable. Core services stay protected.

Keep inventories, identifiers, APKs, captures, research notes and recovery journals private. Synthetic tests do not establish compatibility with a real TV.
