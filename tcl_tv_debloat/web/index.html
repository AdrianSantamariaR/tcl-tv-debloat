Enable debugging on a TCL Google TV
Connection screen example
  1. Open Settings → System → About.
  2. Select Android TV OS build and press OK seven times, until the TV says you are a developer.
  3. Return to System → Developer options and enable USB debugging or the ADB debugging option your TV offers.
  4. Find the TV IP address under System → About → Status or its network settings. Enter it below with the ADB connection port.
  5. Press Connect and scan, then accept Allow USB debugging? on the TV. Press Refresh after authorization if needed; the scan starts for that TV.

Menu names vary by model and firmware. On older Android TVs, look under Device Preferences → About. Network ADB must be supported by the TV; port 5555 is an example, not guaranteed.

Connection troubleshooting

Unauthorized: accept the prompt on the TV, then refresh. Offline: check that the TV is awake and reconnect. Connection refused: check debugging and the connection port. Pairing uses a separate port and code only when offered by the TV. A normal TV USB port may not support an ADB connection.

Set up Basic TV

Basic TV uses no Google sign-in. The Google/TCL system and your Google account remain.

Includes HDMI, live TV and limited preinstalled apps. New app installation, Assistant and casting are unavailable.

Switching an existing Google TV setup requires a factory reset. This erases accounts, apps, app data and TV settings. Recovery cannot undo it. Removing the last Google profile can also trigger a reset.

Instructions only. This app never resets the TV.
  1. Save the sign-in details and TV settings you will need after the reset.
  2. On the TV, follow your model's reset instructions, usually Settings → System → About → Factory reset. Confirm only when ready to erase the data.
  3. During setup, choose Set up basic TV. If unavailable, check your model's official instructions.
  4. Test HDMI, channels, sound, remote, Home and restart. Sideloading and streaming compatibility are untested.
  5. To use this tool again, re-enable debugging, reconnect and scan. Old previews and recovery records may no longer match.

Microphone, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth guidance

Automatic microphone and radio changes are unavailable. Their blocking, restoration and persistence have not been validated on this firmware. These are separate manual options to review on the TV.

  • Microphone: review the global microphone privacy setting when offered, app permissions and Assistant/hands-free controls. A global software block is different from physically disconnecting a microphone. The TV and voice remote may have separate microphones; test ordinary recording and remote voice input. A TV microphone switch does not establish that the remote microphone is disconnected.
  • Wi-Fi and scanning: review Wi-Fi and always-available Wi-Fi scanning separately. A missing/unavailable setting means unknown/default, not off. Wi-Fi off still permits Internet communication over Ethernet. Changing a network used by ADB may disconnect this app; establish recovery access first.
  • Bluetooth: before switching it off, demonstrate an independent control route for navigation, Home, Back, settings, input selection, power and recovery. An attached Bluetooth remote may stop working; infrared fallback is not assumed for this remote. Voice, accessories and wake behavior need checking.
  • Offline HDMI/live TV: disconnect all TV network paths or enforce Internet denial at the router, including IPv4/IPv6. This removes on-TV Internet streaming and online updates; interactive HbbTV may stop working. LAN isolation, DNS filtering and Wi-Fi off do not themselves deny Internet access. Local records may remain for later transmission if reconnected.

No electrical shutdown, hardware-removal procedure, replacement OS or firmware modification is verified for this TV. TCL microphone/Assistant guidance ↗

More privacy controls to review on your TV

These are manual steps; this app does not change Google or HbbTV controls. Menus and available options vary by model, Android version and firmware.

  • Google usage & diagnostics: find Usage & diagnostics in your TV settings, often under Privacy, and turn it off. Apps may collect their own information. Google guidance ↗
  • Advertising ID: on Google TV, open Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID when offered. Reset assigns a new ID. Ads and other identifiers may remain. Google TV steps ↗ · Android TV steps ↗
  • Google account: review Web & App Activity and YouTube History in Activity controls ↗, and personalized ads in My Ad Center ↗.
  • HbbTV: in the TV’s HbbTV settings, review cookie permissions and block third-party cookies when offered. If you do not use broadcast interactive services, consider switching HbbTV off. Cookie storage is separate from TCL reporting settings; changing these two controls does not clear cookies. Interactive broadcast features may stop working.
